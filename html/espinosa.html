Espinosa is a text typeface for novels and long-form reading, drawn from Specimen No. 25 in the Lectura size, roughly 11 to 12 points. Because the model was cut for reading at this size, its proportions already favour a steady rhythm. The revival keeps the sharp, bracketed serifs and adjusts the contrast for contemporary printing. The model comes from the Imprenta Real, the Royal Printing House in Madrid, which by 1799 held one of Europe's largest type collections, sourced in part from Parma and from Jean-Louis Borniche in Paris. Spanish punchcutters such as Antonio Espinosa de los Monteros, after whom the revival is named, gave it a distinctly Iberian character.
